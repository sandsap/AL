01

Executive summary

Home-services contractors run on the phone. When it rings, it is almost always a customer with an urgent, high-value job: a dead furnace, a flooded basement, no power. Yet the typical small contractor misses 25–35% of inbound calls — the owner is on a roof, the one office person is on another line, or it's after 5pm. Every missed call is a booked job that goes to a competitor.

Al is an AI agent that answers every call, 24/7, in a natural voice. It qualifies the caller, books the job directly into the contractor's scheduling software, handles reschedules and FAQs, and follows up on quotes. Unlike a horizontal "AI receptionist," Al is built for the trades: it speaks the vocabulary, integrates deeply with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and Jobber, and is measured on the one number owners care about — jobs booked.

At ~$500/month, Al pays for itself if it captures a single extra job a month. Everything above that is margin the owner keeps.

We reach owners through the channels they already trust — trade-software marketplaces, franchise groups, and peer referral — and compound a defensible data moat from millions of real service calls that let us fine-tune vertical voice models no horizontal competitor can match.

02

The problem

Home services is a $600B+ US market of millions of small businesses, most with fewer than 20 employees. These owners are technicians first, operators second — and their front office is chronically understaffed while the phone is their lifeblood.

Phone is still the channel

60%+ of jobs start with an inbound call. A customer with an emergency calls — they don't fill out a web form.

Missed calls = lost revenue

25–35% of calls go unanswered at small shops, spiking during storms and heat waves — exactly when demand and job value peak.

Labor is scarce & costly

A capable CSR runs $40–55k+ fully loaded, is hard to hire, quits often, and doesn't work nights or weekends.

After-hours is a black hole

30–40% of calls arrive outside 9–5. Voicemail loses most of them for good.

The owner knows they're leaking money on the phone but has no scalable way to fix it. That is the wedge.

03

The solution

Al answers every call and turns it into a booked job. It goes live the same day, configured from the contractor's existing data in under an hour.

  1. 24/7 natural-voice answering. Sub-second latency, interruptible, trade-fluent. Picks up on the first ring — including overflow when staff are busy.
  2. Booking, not just messaging. Reads live availability, books the right job type into the right slot and technician, and writes directly to ServiceTitan / Housecall Pro / Jobber.
  3. Qualification & triage. Separates emergency from routine, captures address, equipment, and problem, and routes true emergencies to on-call staff.
  4. Reschedules, reminders & quote follow-up. Cuts no-shows and revives stale estimates — outbound revenue Al generates on its own.
  5. Owner dashboard. Every call transcribed, summarized, and scored, with a running tally of jobs booked and revenue captured — the ROI, in dollars, front and center.
04

Why now

Voice AI crossed the line

By 2024–25, speech-to-speech latency and naturalness reached the point where callers no longer reliably know they're talking to software — the prerequisite for booking real jobs.

Inference costs are falling

A per-minute voice agent shifts from margin-negative to healthy-margin over our planning horizon.

Deep SMB software base

Field-service platforms now have the APIs and marketplaces that make same-day write-back integrations — and low-CAC distribution — possible.

Worsening labor shortage

"An employee that never quits" is an easy yes for owners who already can't hire.

The window is now: the technology just became good enough, and the horizontal players haven't yet gone deep on any single vertical.

06

Product & technology

Architecture. A real-time voice pipeline — telephony → streaming ASR → an orchestration layer over frontier and fine-tuned LLMs → streaming TTS — wrapped in a deterministic booking engine that enforces business rules and writes to the CRM. Voice is the interface; the booking engine and integrations are the product.

The moat is data + integrations + workflow depth

Vertical data flywheel

Every call, labeled with its outcome, trains models measurably better at this conversation. More customers → more calls → higher booking rate → lower churn. Horizontal rivals lack trade-specific data.

Deep certified integrations

Same-day write-back to ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and Jobber is hard, high-trust engineering. Marketplace listings double as distribution.

Workflow depth

Emergency triage, dispatch rules, multi-location, and financing scripts compound into switching costs.

Outcome accountability

We instrument booked jobs and dollars captured, so the product proves its own ROI and anchors pricing.

08

Go-to-market

ICP: owner-operated HVAC / plumbing / electrical firms, 3–30 technicians, already on a supported field-service platform, feeling the pain of missed calls.

  1. Software marketplaces & integration partnerships. Co-listing and co-marketing with ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, and Jobber for warm, low-CAC distribution.
  2. Franchise & buying groups / PE roll-ups. Land one location, expand to the network. PE-owned platforms are actively hunting exactly this ROI.
  3. Peer channels. Trade Facebook groups, coaching networks, podcasts, and referral incentives. The trades run on word of mouth.
  4. Founder-led outbound to prove the motion before scaling a small inside-sales team.

Land → expand: start on overflow / after-hours (low risk), earn trust with booked jobs, upgrade to the 24/7 primary line and outbound follow-up, then multi-location.
